# Strategy Stack & Founder Playstyles (Phase 9C)

## Overview

The **Strategy Stack** system detects your emerging founder archetype from the decisions you make and evolves it into active gameplay bonuses. There are 10 distinct **Founder Playstyles**, each with its own progression track, synergy effects, and narrative arc.

Your playstyle is never chosen — it emerges from your actions.

---

## The 10 Founder Playstyles

| Playstyle | Title | Core Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `product_led` | Product-Led Growth | Ship quality, let the product speak for itself |
| `enterprise_sales` | Enterprise Sales Machine | Direct sales, enterprise wedge, recurring contracts |
| `regulated_operator` | Regulated Operator | Win through compliance, trust, and regulatory moats |
| `technical_builder` | Technical Moat Builder | Engineering-first, outship and outscale technically |
| `hype_machine` | Hype Machine | Narrative-first, dominate the press cycle and social feeds |
| `cockroach` | Cockroach Founder | Default alive, low burn, long runway, brutal survivability |
| `community_led` | Community-Led Growth | Build loyalty before revenue, users become advocates |
| `rival_killer` | Rival Killer | Competitive aggression, win by defeating rivals |
| `capital_blitzscaler` | Capital Blitzscaler | Raise fast, grow fast, dominate via capital density |
| `trust_builder` | Trust & Transparency Founder | Earn loyalty through honesty, trust compounds over time |

---

## Progression Levels

Each playstyle tracks progress from 0–100 based on accumulated signals:

| Level | Progress Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Dormant | 0–24 | No strategy signal yet |
| Emerging | 25–49 | Strategy is forming — no bonuses yet |
| Active | 50–74 | First synergy unlocks |
| Dominant | 75–100 | Both synergies unlock, defines your archetype |

The **Dominant Playstyle** is your highest-progress stack at ≥50. Up to 2 **Secondary Playstyles** are tracked at ≥25.

## Synergies

Each playstyle has 2 synergies that unlock at Active and Dominant levels. Active synergies apply real bonuses every simulation month.

### product_led
- **PMF Sprint** (Active): +3 product progress, -1 risk per month
- **Launch Readiness** (Dominant): -4 brand risk, +2 product progress per month

### enterprise_sales
- **Enterprise Wedge** (Active): +$1,500 revenue, +1 investor score per month
- **Procurement Credibility** (Dominant): +2 investor score, +$2,000 revenue per month

### regulated_operator
- **Trust Moat** (Active): -2 risk score, -3 brand risk per month
- **Regulated Advantage** (Dominant): +2 investor score, -2 risk score per month

### technical_builder
- **Technical Edge** (Active): +2 product progress, -1 risk per month
- **Scaling Advantage** (Dominant): +3 product progress, -2 risk per month

### hype_machine
- **Viral Loop** (Active): +120 user growth, +4 hype, +$5K valuation per month
- **Narrative Dominance** (Dominant): +80 user growth, +$8K valuation, +2 brand risk per month

### cockroach
- **Runway Discipline** (Active): -$2K burn per month
- **Survive Another Month** (Dominant): -$3K burn, -2 risk score per month

### community_led
- **Community Flywheel** (Active): +80 user growth, +3 social trust per month
- **Brand Shield** (Dominant): +60 user growth, -5 brand risk per month

### rival_killer
- **Arena Predator** (Active): +1 investor score, -1 risk score per month
- **Competitive Clarity** (Dominant): +2 investor score, +$5K valuation per month

### capital_blitzscaler
- **Capital Momentum** (Active): +2 investor score, +$10K valuation per month
- **Boardroom Leverage** (Dominant): +3 investor score, +$15K valuation per month

### trust_builder
- **Brand Shield** (Active): +4 social trust, -4 brand risk per month
- **Transparency Credibility** (Dominant): +5 social trust, +1 investor score, -5 brand risk per month

## Balance Caps

Strategy synergies are hard-capped per month to prevent snowballing:

| Effect | Max Change |
|---|---|
| Revenue | ±$3,000 |
| Product Progress | ±5 |
| Investor Score | ±3 |
| Risk Score | ±4 |
| User Growth | ±200 |
| Valuation | ±$20,000 |
| Burn | -$3,000 to +$1,500 |
| Social Hype | ±6 |
| Social Trust | ±5 |
| Brand Risk | ±8 |

## Conflict Penalties

Some playstyle combinations create trade-off tensions:

| Conflict | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Hype Machine (≥50) + product progress < 40 | +3 brand risk per month |
| Rival Killer (≥50) + social trust < 35 | +2 brand risk per month |
| Cockroach (≥25) + Capital Blitzscaler (≥25) | +1 brand risk per month |

## Warnings & Recommendations

The Strategy page surfaces **warnings** and **recommendations** based on your current stack state:

**Warnings** (things going wrong):
- Hype outpacing product (backlash risk)
- High brand risk with active hype strategy
- Conflicting Cockroach + Hype Machine signals
- Conflicting Cockroach + Capital Blitzscaler signals
- Rival Killer escalating rivalry retaliation
- Rival Killer with low social trust
- Multiple launches with low product progress
- Dominant Hype Machine with zero product foundation

**Recommendations** (actions to take):
- Unlock next synergy threshold (specific to near-50 stacks)
- Transparency post when brand risk > 50
- Product-building actions when product progress < 40
- Enterprise revenue actions when enterprise emerging but revenue is low
- Rival counter-actions when rivalry is escalating

## Archetype Summary (End of Run)

When your run ends (completed or dead), a **Founder Archetype Summary** is generated and stored. It includes:
- Your dominant archetype title
- A narrative describing how you played
- Your specific strengths and weaknesses
- A won/lost run narrative reflecting the outcome

The summary appears on the Strategy page and is saved to your startup's permanent record.

## Architecture Notes (Engineering)

- Signals stored as `strategySignals Json[]` on the `SocialState` table — no new tables
- All strategy state (stacks, levels, synergies, warnings) computed in-memory from signals
- Signal deduplication by `sourceId`: `"{source}:{actionId}:m{month}:{playstyle}"`
- Signal cap: 500 per startup (oldest dropped)
- Effects applied after the Rival layer, before the death check in `runSimulationMonth`
- 100% deterministic — no external APIs, no randomness beyond existing simulation RNG
